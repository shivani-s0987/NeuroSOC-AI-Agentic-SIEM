# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

At **NeuroSOC**, we are committed to fostering a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community where contributors, security researchers, developers, and learners can collaborate in a positive and professional environment.

We value diversity, constructive collaboration, and mutual respect.

---

## 🌟 Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Treating everyone with professionalism and respect
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Providing constructive and thoughtful feedback
- Respecting different perspectives and experiences
- Sharing knowledge openly and helping others learn
- Focusing on improving the project and supporting the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Offensive, abusive, or hateful language
- Trolling, intimidation, or disruptive conduct
- Sharing private or confidential information without permission
- Any behavior that creates an unsafe or unwelcoming environment

---

## 🚨 Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it by opening a GitHub Issue or contacting the project maintainers privately.

All reports will be handled respectfully, fairly, and with appropriate confidentiality.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for maintaining a healthy community and may take appropriate action when unacceptable behavior occurs, including warnings, temporary restrictions, or removal from the project when necessary.

---

## ❤️ Our Community

By participating in **NeuroSOC**, you agree to uphold this Code of Conduct and contribute to a collaborative, respectful, and inclusive open-source community.

Together, we can build better security tools—and a stronger cybersecurity community.
